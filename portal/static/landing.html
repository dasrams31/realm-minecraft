ABOUT THE REALM

Muse SMP is now home to a massive shogunate-era metropolis — 400+ hand-built Japanese buildings protected by three colossal Attack-on-Titan-style walls. Explore the 5-story castle keep, the golden-pillared Shogun Palace, a sumo arena, kabuki theater, samurai academy, and night market.

85 NPCs bring the city to life: samurai guards at the gates, archers in the towers, merchants in the market, and villagers in the streets. Spawn at the castle plaza, wander the cherry-blossom avenues, or relax in the giant city park.

No premium Minecraft account needed: TLauncher works. Cheats allowed, PvP on, and no sudden world resets. Build your base, challenge your friends to a duel, or just wander around enjoying the blocky sunset.

This realm is hand-maintained by the admin — if something's wrong, just report it.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Can TLauncher / cracked MC join?
Yes. The server doesn't require a premium account. But you must register a password with /register when you first join, then /login every time after.
Which Minecraft version do I need?
The latest one (26.3). If yours is older, update via your launcher first — otherwise you'll be rejected with an "outdated" message.
Are cheats allowed?
Yes. Commands like /gamemode, /give, and /tp are free to use. Just don't use them to grief or ruin others' game.
What if I forget my AuthMe password?
Contact the admin — passwords can be reset from the portal. Never share your password with anyone.
Is my progress safe if I switch computers?
Yes, as long as you use the same username and remember your AuthMe password. Data is stored on the server.
When is the server online?
24/7, except for brief maintenance/restarts. Live status is shown at the top of this page.
Who are Muse and MuseAFK?
Muse is a helper bot at spawn — chat with it, or type tpa <player> to request a teleport. MuseAFK keeps chunks loaded for farms — type summon in chat to teleport it to you.
Is the village safe from monsters?
Yes! The entire village is a protected zone — monsters can't spawn there and creepers can't explode. The rest of the world is normal survival.
